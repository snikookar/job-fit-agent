# Grocery Price Forecasting

University group project, 2018. Forecasting weekly grocery prices for 50 products.

## Approach

- Built features from historical prices, holidays and weather data with pandas.
- Compared ARIMA, gradient boosted trees (LightGBM) and a small LSTM in PyTorch.
- LightGBM had the lowest error (MAPE 6.4%) and was the model we presented.

I owned the data pipeline and the LightGBM model, and wrote the evaluation section of the report.
